First release of Himitsu, a privacy-first fork of RPClient.

- Encrypted databases, files and preferences; keys protected by the Android Keystore, optional recovery code.
- App lock with biometrics or the device credential.
- On-device models with llama.cpp, optional OpenCL GPU, verified downloads.
- Optional disguise as a calculator, compass or unit converter (discretion, not security; limits shown in the app).
- New night theme.
- No telemetry, no ads, no Google services.
